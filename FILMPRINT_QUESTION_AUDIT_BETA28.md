# FILMPRINT Question Audit — Beta 28

## Verdict
The 30-question / 15-axis structure is worth keeping. It is short enough to finish, every axis has one direct and one reverse-coded signal, and the scoring already down-weights Neutral-heavy or contradictory pairs.

Beta 28 keeps the same 15 axes and the same positive/reverse mapping, but rewrites the prompts to reduce semantic overlap and accidental measurement of adjacent traits.

## Main fixes
- REALITY BEND now measures whether the film world itself departs from recognizable reality.
- DREAM LOGIC now measures associative / intuitive cause-and-effect. This separates it more clearly from REALITY BEND and COMPLEXITY.
- SOLITUDE no longer treats "lively group" as a simple opposite of loneliness; it now contrasts inward isolation with relational/ensemble energy.
- NOSTALGIA no longer uses "modern and forward-looking" as the reverse item, because a viewer can enjoy both modern cinema and nostalgia. The reverse now measures whether evoking the past actually matters to the viewer.
- DARKNESS now contrasts sustained bleak/morally abrasive material with a preference for emotional breathing room, rather than only asking whether the viewer feels "wrecked" afterward.
- HUMOR now distinguishes tonal elasticity from sustained seriousness.
- INTIMACY now separates interior character focus from scale/external conflict more precisely.
- VISUAL STYLE, COMPLEXITY, ACTION, HORROR, PACE and INTENSITY received wording cleanup to make each item target one primary construct.

## Structural check
- Questions: 30
- Axes: 15
- Signals per axis: 2
- Every axis: 1 forward + 1 reverse-coded item
- Neutral remains a true zero-preference response.
- Contradictory paired answers still reduce that axis's weight rather than creating false confidence.

## Known limitation
Two items per axis is a deliberately compact product design, not a psychometrically validated personality inventory. For CineGenome's goal — an interpretable cinematic-taste matcher — 30 signals is a good balance. If the candidate pool grows far beyond roughly 500–1000 films, the next meaningful upgrade should be adaptive follow-up questions or extra discriminating axes rather than simply adding more generic prompts.
