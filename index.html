DO NOT FOLLOW
THE LAB IN ORDER.

The map is for orientation, not obedience.

Start with a film you know too well. The Scanner will tell you what the archive thinks it sees. Crossbreed and Mutation are where you are allowed to disagree with it. Atlas shows where the evidence sits. Bloodline shows what sits close enough to make you suspicious.

Do not force missing evidence into a number. UNKNOWN is not zero. PROVISIONAL is not truth. A clean readout can still be an interpretation. If an instrument sounds certain, read the label twice.

The indexed rooms are only the rooms I was able to name. Other channels reveal themselves when you use the lab instead of treating it like a menu. Some are daily. Some remember this browser. Some involve other visitors. A few are undocumented on purpose.

Save whatever is worth returning to. Leave notes when you find something strange. Compare signals. Break combinations. The lab is more useful when the operator has bad ideas and keeps records.

I have been trying to determine whether this place has an exit or only a return path. The CineGenome logo will always bring you back to the board. That is not the same thing.
